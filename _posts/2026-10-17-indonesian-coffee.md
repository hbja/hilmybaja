### Writing getaway to Indonesia

I went back to Indonesia for holidays during the summer. We were there for 2 months. *Why so long?* You might ask. My cousin was getting married mid-June and Zahra's cousin was getting married late-July. Perfect excuse to be in Indonesia in-between these events.  
Being there for 2 months was quite the break. But not so much of a break for me as I spent most of it sitting down alone, working on my thesis. I underestimated the amount of `thinking` (compared to writing) that goes into writing the *introduction* and *synthesis* of a PhD thesis. I remember spending a lot of time reading and contemplating while looking at the screen of my laptop.

While not in front of the laptop, I had a lot of opportunities to try a lot of Indonesian coffee. Especially during my week-trip to Bandung. I might've had 10 shots of coffee each day. I thoroughly enjoyed it.

### *I thought coffee was only bean juice*

There was this hype for ***[Mont Blanc](https://www.taste.com.au/food-news/mont-blanc-coffee-why-everyone-drinking-them/cx3zjnqe)*** iced coffee. It's basically cold-brewed coffee topped with sweet cold whipping foam and orange zest, popularised by an Australian café. I had one. This is what it looks like (personal picture I took below):

![IMG_0801.jpeg](/images/IMG_0801.jpeg)

I wanted to try it to see what it was all about. It was, meh. About cold coffee, I still much prefer a classic iced americano.

I had a chance to try a [Panama Gesha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geisha_(coffee)), cultivated from Indonesia! If you don't know, Panama Gesha is quite a popular and well-regarded coffee type, well-known (especially in the community) for its unique taste.

![IMG_0893.jpeg](blob:https:/app.pagescms.org/ee9f69d7-255e-4aed-a19b-637e3e97b0e6)

I thoroughly enjoyed this iced V60 that was served to me in Makmur Jaya, Bandung. I also took a photo of the receipt I see. It was a 7 euro cup; worth every penny.

Also had a classic Cortado with some Indonesian arabica blend.

![IMG_0924.jpeg](/images/IMG_0924.jpeg)

### Some devices are made to trick your senses to make you feel that certain things are better than they *actually* are

I realised that I didn't take a picture of this interesting Sundanese Ciwidey arabica pour-over I had. it was in a cup with two colours, half white, half pink, with different shaped spouts on either side. Maybe that wasn't the best description; let me how you a stock picture:

![image.png](/images/image.png)

Somehow---not sure if placebo, maybe it was---drinking from either spout made the same drink taste different! It was quite a nice experience. As I remember, drinking from the round spout had a deeper and smoother taste. Drinking from the sharper spout had a more acidic and up-your-nose taste. Not sure if it's real, but I did indeed experience this.

It was a great time! I unfortunately did not write a single sentence of my thesis while being caffeinated with 10 shots of coffee. I wrote all that with a normal amount of caffeine in my system :)
